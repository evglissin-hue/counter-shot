Controls

Click
Take the gun, then fire. Esc frees the mouse
Mouse, W A S D
Aim. Heavy guns turn slowly; with the keys a gun gathers speed and coasts, so tap to correct. The mortar: up and down look farther or nearer
Shift
Fine aim
Right mouse, Z
Zoom (scroll sets it)
Space
Fire. The mortar: hold to charge, let go to fire. The ring on the ground is where it lands in still air
1 to 8, Q, E
Switch gun (Q and E skip empty slots)
R
Long gun spin: top, back, left, right
Guns
Knock one out: bring down the masonry under it, hit it square, sweep its crew with chain shot, or shell it. It is out 30 s, or until masons rebuild under it (20 gold to whoever does it)
[ ], Alt+scroll
Mortar fuse: on impact, or a delay (shorter than the flight bursts in the air)
V
Shot camera: follow your shot (the mortar rides its shell)
K
Impact cam: a small view of where your shot lands, a second before it does. Click it to swap views
B
Build menu: mills, barracks, a foundry, guns for empty slots, upgrades
G
Hold to see the minimap twice its size (or point at it)
L
Route of your next waves: left, centre, right
T, F
T mark where your shot landed; F and a number: fire for effect
Ctrl+1, 2, 3
Waves: advance on the gate, hold at midfield, push the breach
Alt+1 to 4
Masons' repairs on or off: gate, walls, towers, keep
Enter
Order line: type orders in plain words ("mill, then waves left"), Enter sends, Esc closes. Aiming goes on behind it
Y (hold)
Speak an order; let go to send it
Tab
Overview (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom)
N
Where minions can walk: green open, amber cut off, red blocked
M, P
Sound on or off; pause (not against a friend)
Gamepad
Left stick aim, right stick fine aim, RT fire, LT zoom, bumpers switch gun, D-pad fuse (up, down), orders (left), route (right), X spin, Y shot camera, B back, View overview
