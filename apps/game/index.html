Controls

Click
Take the gun (mouse aims), then fire
Mouse, W A S D
Aim. Heavy guns turn slowly: lead your target. With the keys a gun gathers speed while you hold them and coasts to a stop: tap for small corrections. Aim help shows the first part of each flight, your last shot's trail and where it landed: correct from there, and mind the wind (it gusts: watch the gauge, the flags and the smoke). No two shots fly quite alike. The mortar is aimed from an observer's view, centred on where the shell would land in still air; forward sends it farther
Shift
Fine aim
Right mouse, Z
Zoom the sight or the observer's view (scroll sets it)
Space
Fire
1 to 4, Q, E
Switch weapon
[ ] or Alt+scroll
Mortar fuse. Starts on impact; a fuse shorter than the flight bursts in the air (the arc stops there), a longer one lets the shell bounce first
R
Long gun spin: top, back, left, right
V
Shot camera for this weapon: follow your shot (slow motion at impact if you turn it on below). On for the mortar: ride the shell down
Click, Space, Enter
While riding a shell: back to the mortar
M
Sound on or off (volume below)
Tab
Overview (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom)
N
Show where minions can walk: green from your gate, sandy over rubble and the fallen, amber cut off, blue water, red in the way. Blow a bridge and watch its crossing close
Ctrl+1, 2, 3
Versus AI: orders for all your waves (or the buttons in the match bar). Advance on the enemy gate (they batter it down), Hold at the midfield markers short of the bridges, Push the breach (the nearest gap in the enemy walls: knock one open at the foot of a wall)
L
Versus AI: the route of your next waves (left flank, centre road, right flank). A wave whose bridge is down takes the nearest one standing
Alt+1, 2, 3, 4
Versus AI: your masons' repairs on or off for the gate, walls, towers and keep (or the chips in the match bar). Each stone costs gold; masons rebuild the lowest gap first, and run from enemy pikemen. Shoot the enemy's masons to keep a breach open
Storming
Win by holding the enemy courtyard for 20 seconds with more men inside than its defenders. Each courtyard keeps a garrison of four; a barracks under attack musters its wave inside. Every other wave carries a ladder: shoot the crews before they reach your wall
Gamepad
Left stick aims, right stick fine aim, RT fire, LT zoom, bumpers switch, D-pad up and down fuse, D-pad left orders, D-pad right route, X spin, Y shot camera, B back from the shot camera, View overview
